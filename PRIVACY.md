# Privacy Policy

This is a personal, single-user automation project ("Raptar"). It is not offered to the public.

- The app requests permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (youtube.upload scope) and nothing else.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any other user.
- Credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only in the owner's private GitHub Secrets.
